# 🚀 Tech, Scales & Rockets
**Tech Unlocks Bosses ➔ Bosses Unlock Tech**

> **Short Summary:** *Tech, Scales & Rockets* is an expert-style progression modpack for NeoForge 1.21.1 where industrial automation, dragon hunting, and interstellar travel are inextricably bound: **Tech unlocks Bosses, and Bosses unlock Tech.**

---

Tired of kitchen-sink modpacks where you rush a Digital Miner and a 32-channel ME Controller in the first two hours, rendering 90% of the game's bosses and dimensions pointless? 

Welcome to **Tech, Scales & Rockets**—a custom-crafted, 8-phase progression modpack for **Minecraft 1.21.1 (NeoForge)**. In this pack, you cannot simply brute-force your way through technology with raw iron and osmium. Every major technological leap requires trophies torn from ancient bosses, mythical dragons, and planetary expeditions.

---

## ⚙️ Core Design Pillars

* **No Early-Game Tech Rush:** In Phase 0, Mekanism machines are strictly limited to **1-slot basic machines**, and Applied Energistics 2 is restricted to an **8-channel Ad-Hoc network** (no ME Controller!). To expand your factory to 3, 5, 7, and 9 parallel slots—or unlock the **ME Controller** and **Digital Miner**—you must venture into the **Twilight Forest** and claim the trophies of its guardians.
* **The Sacred Armor Lineage:** Overpowered side-armors (*MekaSuit, Dragonsteel, Ignitium*) have been disabled. Your gear follows a single, sacred smithing evolution: **Netherite ➔ Allthemodium ➔ Vibranium ➔ Unobtainium**. Every enchantment, Apotheosis gem socket, and affix you forge onto your Netherite armor carries over all the way to endgame Godhood!
* **Safe Overworld, Deadly Frontiers:** Fire, Lightning, and Ice Dragons have been exiled from the Overworld. Your main base is safe—but when you need Dragon Scales and Dragon Blood to forge **Infinite Wireless AE2**, **Rocket Fuel**, and **Cataclysm Boss Keys**, you will have to hunt them down in **The Other** and across the frozen wastes of **Glacio**.
* **Space-Age Boss Keys:** The slumbering titans of *L_Ender's Cataclysm* cannot be awakened early. Their summoning altars require planetary metals (**Desh, Ostrum, Calorite**) mined from the Moon, Mars, and Venus/Glacio.
* **The Iron Curtain of Unobtainium:** Raw Unobtainium defies conventional physics—it cannot be smelted in any furnace or doubled in Mekanism lines. The only way to refine **Unobtainium Ingots** is inside the **Mekanism Combiner**, fusing Raw Unobtainium with **Ignitium** or **Void Cores** dropped by Cataclysm bosses.
* **Antimatter Endgame:** Build a 24/7 solar-powered **SPS Antimatter Reactor** in Earth Orbit to synthesize a **Pellet of Antimatter**—the sole key capable of awakening the final mechanical titan, **The Harbinger**, and unlocking true **Creative Flight (Angel Ring)** and the **Infinity Backpack Upgrade**.

---

## 🗺️ The 8 Phases of Progression

1. **Phase 0: Spark of Industry** – Vanilla roots, 1-slot Mekanism machines, Storage Drawers, and an 8-channel Ad-Hoc AE2 network.
2. **Phase 1: Twilight Awakening** – Slay the *Naga* and *Lich* to unlock **3-Slot Basic Factories** and the **Mekanism Jetpack**. Conquer the *Hydra*, *Ur-Ghast*, and *Alpha Yeti* to unlock the **ME Controller**, **Digital Miner**, and the **Wither Skull Ritual**.
3. **Phase 2: Echoes & Stars** – Defeat the *Wither* and the *Warden* to claim your first **Allthemodium**, construct the **Teleport Pad**, enter the **Mining Dimension**, and upgrade to **5-Slot Advanced Factories** & **Flux Networks**.
4. **Phase 3: Realm of Dragons** – Travel to **The Other**, hunt Fire & Lightning Dragons, mine **Vibranium**, and forge the **Quantum Bridge Card** for infinite cross-dimensional AE2 access.
5. **Phase 4: Cosmic Odyssey** – Synthesize rocket fuel in the Mekanism Combiner and launch across the **Moon, Earth Orbit, Mars, Venus, and Glacio**. Unlock **7-Slot Elite Factories** and hunt **Ice Dragons**.
6. **Phase 5: Cataclysm Unleashed** – Use planetary metals and dragon blood to awaken *Ancient Remnant, Ignis, Netherite Monstrosity, The Leviathan, Maledictus,* and the *Ender Guardian*.
7. **Phase 6: Alchemical Godhood** – Fuse **Unobtainium** in the Combiner, forge **Unobtainium Armor**, and unlock **9-Slot Ultimate Factories**, **ExtendedAE Assembler Matrices**, and the **Tempad**.
8. **Phase 7: Antimatter & The Masterchef** – Synthesize **Antimatter**, awaken **The Harbinger**, cook the divine **Allthemodium Apple** in the *Farmer's Delight* Cooking Pot, and claim your **Angel Ring**, **Ore Laser Base**, and **Infinity Upgrade**!

## ✨ Features & Quality of Life

* **Custom 9-Chapter FTB Questbook:** Over 90 handcrafted quests guiding you through every custom mechanic, boss fight, and automation tip.
* **5-Row Vitality System:** Progress through *Baubley Heart Canisters* (Red, Yellow, Green, Blue) with custom boss drop tables tailored to each phase.
* **Tiered Apothic Enchanting:** Bookshelves are integrated into boss progression, scaling smoothly from *Hellshelves* to *Draconic Endshelves*.
* **Anti-Cheese Protection:** Bosses are blacklisted from capturing and spawners, and divine/planetary ores are protected from automated Digital Miner strip-mining until you reach the Phase 7 Void Laser Drill.
